QuestPilot Privacy Policy

Customer Support

For QuestPilot help, feedback or accessibility requests, email roomary42@gmail.com.

For a bug, include your device model, iOS version, QuestPilot version if known, and the steps that led to the problem. Leave out passwords, recovery codes, personal notes and backup files.

Save or restore progress

Open Settings and choose Export progress to make a backup, or Import progress to restore one. Restore replaces the current saved progress after confirmation. Keep a backup you can access before changing devices or resetting progress.

Locations and content updates

With Essential Spoiler Shield, location details can be hidden. If you want to see more, you can choose a higher detail level in Settings. Map shows a location list when verified coordinates are unavailable.

Look in Settings for the installed content version and update status. You can continue using installed content offline.

App Store billing

Apple handles App Store billing and refund requests. Get help with a refund from Apple.

QuestPilot is an independent, unofficial companion app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive.
