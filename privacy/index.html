Privacy Policy

Last updated November 16, 2026

This policy covers the QuestPilot iOS app and its Privacy Policy and Customer Support pages. QuestPilot works without an app account and does not integrate advertising or app analytics.

Data on your device

QuestPilot keeps progress, visits, favorites, personal notes and settings in its local database. The app uses them to show your saved information and apply your preferences. QuestPilot does not sync that database to its own servers or read gameplay or game accounts.

Local data stays on your device until you edit or remove it. Use Reset all progress in Settings to clear your saved journey and entries, including notes and favorites. The reset keeps preferences. To remove the app’s remaining local data, use Delete App in iOS. Your device’s backup settings may affect whether it keeps a copy of app data.

Progress backups

When you export or import progress, QuestPilot uses a backup file that can contain your notes and other saved progress. The app may create a local cache copy while handling the file. If you use the share sheet or save the file to another service, that destination handles the copy under its own settings and terms. Remove exported copies from the locations you chose if you no longer want them there. Clearing progress inside QuestPilot does not delete those copies.

Online content checks

When online, the production app checks for signed content updates at startup and when you request a check. It fetches a manifest and, when needed, a content pack over HTTPS using Google Firebase Hosting. These requests do not send your notes, favorites, settings or progress. Firebase Hosting receives connection information such as your IP address. Google documents using incoming IPs for abuse prevention and usage analysis and retaining the IP data for a few months. You can use installed content and local tracking offline to avoid sending new update requests.

Website and support

These public pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security. The pages themselves contain no tracking scripts, forms or embedded third-party content.

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include. We use that information to respond, investigate your issue and handle privacy requests. We keep correspondence as needed for those purposes and applicable obligations. Please omit passwords, recovery codes and personal notes or backup files.

App Store purchases

Apple handles App Store purchases, billing details and refund requests under the App Store privacy notice. QuestPilot does not ask for payment card details or process payments inside the app.
